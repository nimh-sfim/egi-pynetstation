# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for
everyone who participates in this project, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to communicate constructively, respect differing
viewpoints and experiences, give and accept feedback in good faith, and focus
on what is best for the research community.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publication
of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers at
`peter.molfese@nih.gov`. Reports will be handled promptly and respectfully.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that do not
follow this Code of Conduct.
